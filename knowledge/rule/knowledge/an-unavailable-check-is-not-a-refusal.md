  - Given a case that declares no hypothesis and a registry that cannot be reached, when it is published, then the refusal that check produced is answered and the unavailable contract check is answered beside it, and the case does not publish.
  - Given that same case, when it is published, then no refusal names the unavailability, because what this rule forbids is the unavailability wearing a refusal's shape and nothing else.

## What it is

A refusal names a rule the case offended, and here the case may be perfect, so answering with one would state something about the case that nothing established.
An infrastructure failure read as a fact of the domain is the pathology the rest of the system exists to prevent, and this is the same discipline that keeps having no data, a failed judgement and an exhausted deadline apart in an investigation.
What this forbids is the unavailability wearing a refusal's shape, and only that: a refusal another check produced states a fact about the case that is established, and silencing it because the registry is out is the very confusion this rule exists to prevent, committed in the other direction.

## Rules

A case does not publish while the contract check cannot be decided.
A validation answers with every refusal its checks produced, and the unavailable contract check is answered beside them rather than in place of them.
