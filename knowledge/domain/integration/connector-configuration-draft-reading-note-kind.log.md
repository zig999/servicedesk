  unstated: The material names the conditions a draft reads past or reads through -- a default response, a status-range key, a non-JSON success body, an envelope, united variants, a repeated field name, no responses, no success schema -- and leaves open whether they join the unresolved-reason enumeration or become an element of their own, naming no closed set.
