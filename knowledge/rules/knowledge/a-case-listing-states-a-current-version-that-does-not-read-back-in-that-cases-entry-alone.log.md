Recorded as a new policy over domain/knowledge/case, domain/knowledge/case-version and domain/knowledge/case-summary, eventual.
  why: A case's summary is derived from that case's own versions alone, so one case's version failing validation is a fact about that entry and about no other, and the material's own scope names a single invalid case taking the whole listing down as the defect rather than the design; the statement is what sends the curator to correct that version, where any summary fact presented beside it would state as the case's current content exactly what validation has just declined to read back as a case.
