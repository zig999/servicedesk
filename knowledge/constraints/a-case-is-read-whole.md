---
statement: A case is read whole — its root, its hypotheses and their resolutions and referrals in one transaction — or not at all; no read returns part of a case.
scope: knowledge
fitness: The case store's read returns a complete aggregate or nothing, verifiable in its own tests.
---

## Description

The aggregate boundary outlives the one document that used to be it: hypotheses, resolutions and referrals now sit in relations of their own, and what kept them arriving together was the document rather than a decision, so the decision is stated here.
A partially loaded case is a case whose collection plan is short and whose precedence order has holes, and neither announces itself — resolve-outcome would answer from whichever hypotheses happened to arrive.
