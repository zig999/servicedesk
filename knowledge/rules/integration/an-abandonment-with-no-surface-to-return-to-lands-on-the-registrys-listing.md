## Description

`an-abandoned-capability-registration-entry-registers-nothing` and `a-connector-configuration-authoring-may-be-abandoned-without-registering` each state the abandonment's destination as the surface the authoring was reached from, and neither states a destination for an authoring surface that was reached from nothing.
That reading is not an edge of these registries but an ordinary one: `read-capability-by-identity` was published for a surface that loads on first navigation or a page refresh, `read-connector-configuration` serves the same shape under a connector name, and both authoring surfaces are addressed the same way, so an operator opens one at its address or reloads it as a matter of course.
On that reading the stated destination names nothing at all, and where the operator lands would fall to whatever an interface happened to do — including doing nothing, which leaves the operator exactly where the abandonment is supposed to take them out of.

Which surfaces this reaches, including an authoring surface widened to a standing registration's own unsubmitted edit, is `an-authoring-surface-includes-an-unsubmitted-edit-of-a-standing-registration`'s own — the second half of that same silence, stated there with this one.

The listing answers, and answering is what the destination has to do.
`list-capabilities` and `list-connector-configurations` answer from what is currently registered, and `constraints/listings-are-paged` makes each answer a page carrying its data and the total currently held — a total of zero where nothing is registered yet — so neither read refuses on any reading.
That is the property the two abandonment rules chose the reached-from surface for, a surface having answered once already, and it is exactly what a destination keyed on the identity being authored or presented lacks: `constraints/the-capability-identity-read-refuses-an-unregistered-identity` and `a-connector-configuration-read-by-an-unregistered-name-is-refused` refuse the identity an abandoned creation leaves behind, which is why that destination is refused here for the same reason it is refused there.

This does not reopen the reading the specification already discarded.
The decision log records return to the listing as an earlier reading of these two rules that the analysis replaced, because it privileged the listing over the surface the operator came from and moved an operator who had been working somewhere else away from it.
That reason has no purchase on a surface reached from nowhere: there is no place the operator was working to be taken from, and no arrival to undo.
The listing is stated here as the destination where no reached-from surface exists and nowhere else; where one exists, the two rules' own answer stands exactly as it stands, so one gesture keeps one answer.

Nor may the leaving leave the operator on the surface.
`a-connector-configuration-authoring-may-be-abandoned-without-registering` states that no operator is ever left on a surface whose only other exit is a registration they have decided against, and staying is already a different act with a rule of its own: `a-loaded-registration-edit-may-be-discarded-without-leaving-the-surface` returns the fields to the read content and keeps the operator there, and it takes a further, explicit act the abandonment does not take.
Landing an abandonment back on the surface it left would collapse two acts this specification tells apart and would have one gesture owe a confirmation and not owe it at once.
That distinction is why the widened reach above changes nothing about the act that stays: a surface presenting a registration and holding an unsubmitted edit offers both, and they remain two acts, one landing the operator elsewhere and one leaving them in place and editing.

The listing is also where these surfaces already owe the operator a route.
`a-single-capability-surface-offers-a-route-to-the-capabilities-listing` and `a-connector-configuration-surface-offers-a-route-to-the-listing` owe a route to these very listings on every reading of an authoring surface, of a surface presenting one registration, and of whatever surface either was reached from, so landing there gives the operator no reach they did not already have and takes nothing from either obligation, which stands untouched; a shared destination on a reading reached from nothing leaves them two acts, exactly as `a-single-registration-surface-offers-a-return-to-its-origin-on-every-reading` already reads it.
The objection `a-successful-capability-registration-lands-on-the-capabilitys-own-surface` and `a-successful-connector-registration-lands-on-the-configurations-own-surface` raise against the listing — that which page a new registration falls on is `listings-are-paged`'s own answer, so the operator would land where their own registration is not reliably in view — does not reach an abandonment, which registers nothing and leaves no registration that has to be in view.

One fact decided once for both registries, on the reading `a-submitted-registration-states-its-outcome-to-the-operator` and `a-loaded-registration-edit-may-be-discarded-without-leaving-the-surface` already took over this same pair.
Consistency is eventual because the statement holds over both aggregate roots at once and because what the operator lands on is the answer of a listing read issued separately to a registry, which the abandoned surface never held.
Which control carries the abandonment, its wording and where it sits are form and belong to the interface, not here, exactly as both abandonment rules close.
Whether the leaving registers anything, or leaves the operator on the surface or on a read keyed on the identity, is `an-abandonment-with-no-surface-to-return-to-registers-nothing`'s own.
Surfaces over any other subject are untouched: the same silence over a case version's editing or a hypothesis revision's composition would be its own fact about its own element.
