## What it is

Precedence is the knowledge a case declares, which is why resolving it belongs to the case and not to whatever runs it.
No validator can check this, and it is one of two things the material leaves to human review.
Two hypotheses confirming in the same investigation is the signal that the order is wrong, and it is only visible because every hypothesis is judged even after one has confirmed.
