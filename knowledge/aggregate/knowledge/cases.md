## What it is

Everything a curator edits about one diagnostic procedure changes together and is published together.
Duplicating a hypothesis between two cases is accepted as cheaper than extracting it into a construct of its own, until the third duplication.

## Rules

A case is published whole or not at all, and the contract checks run over the whole.
