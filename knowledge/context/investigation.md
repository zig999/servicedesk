## What it is

An investigation is what an attendant gets while a customer waits on the line, and it exists once and is never changed afterwards.
It collects every concept the case plans, asks for one judgement per hypothesis, and then asks the case itself which outcome follows.
It decides nothing the case has not already decided, which is why the outcome and the referral can never be produced here.
The guarantee it offers about a judgement is not that the judgement is correct but that it is cited and complete.

## Rules

Every hypothesis the case declares gets an evaluation, and silence is not one.
Nothing is answered to the requester before the investigation has a record.
A stage that exhausts its deadline records that fact and the investigation continues.
