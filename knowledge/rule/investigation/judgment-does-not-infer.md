## What it is

A judgement sees only its own hypothesis's criterion and its own hypothesis's evidence, and the evidence arrives as delimited data rather than as instruction.
External data reaching a judgement cannot make it act, since nothing it can call exists, but a free text field can still make it judge wrongly.

## Rules

None.
