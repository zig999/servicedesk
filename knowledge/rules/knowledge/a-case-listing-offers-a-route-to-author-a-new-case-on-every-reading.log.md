  unstated: Whether the surface presenting the listing of every case offers a curator a route to author a new case, and whether that offer holds while the listing's own read has not yet answered, has failed, or has answered no case. contracts/knowledge/case-query publishes list-cases and contracts/knowledge/case-lifecycle publishes create-draft, rules/knowledge/a-case-listing-answers-cases-in-slug-order fixes the order that listing answers in, and rules/knowledge/a-listed-case-version-offers-a-route-to-its-own-manifest states a route from a listing of one case's versions onward — but no node states any route from the listing of every case to the authoring of a new one, so both the offer's existence and its conditionality fell to whatever the interface happened to render.
  decided: The listing of every case offers the curator a route to author a new case on every reading of that listing, its presence turning on nothing about the read backing the listing — including a read that has not yet answered, a read that failed, and a read that answered no case at all. Recorded as a new invariant over domain/knowledge/case.
  why: 'This specification has already answered this exact shape once, for the sibling registry, in rules/integration/a-connector-configurations-listing-offers-a-route-to-author-a-new-configuration-on-every-reading, and deciding it the other way here would give one question two answers across two listings. The reasoning carries over unchanged: the listing''s read answers which cases are stored and nothing about whether a new one may be authored, so a route to author gated behind that read''s state refuses the curator an act for a reason unrelated to it, and the reading that answered no case is the one where the route is needed most — a curator who has never authored a case meets exactly that reading. The presence-turns-on-nothing-further construction is the one rules/integration/a-single-capability-surface-offers-a-route-to-the-capabilities-listing and rules/integration/a-connector-configuration-surface-offers-a-route-to-the-listing already use for a route whose availability must not read as conditional, each on the ground that the conditional readings are the ones with nothing else to act on. A new node rather than an addition to a-case-listing-answers-cases-in-slug-order, whose statement fixes the order the listing answers in and introduces no surface obligation, and rather than to a-listed-case-version-offers-a-route-to-its-own-manifest, whose listing is one case''s versions and whose destination is a manifest. The control, its wording and its placement are left undecided deliberately, on the standing boundary four rules of this specification already hold. Corroboration: the intake material reports the delivered listing rendering a permanently disabled create control and no authoring route existing at all, so the absence of any node is what the delivered surface has been standing on; the value decided rests on the specification''s own siblings, so a reviewer who rejects it rejects that reasoning rather than the code.'
