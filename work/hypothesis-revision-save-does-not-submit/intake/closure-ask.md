# The ask to close this initiative

Stated by the human, asked as "parar e fechar a iniciativa" and given this why, in their own words:

> As duas telas críticas estão corrigidas; o resto espera.

The reading offered alongside it, and accepted with it: the initiative delivered what hurt — the
hypothesis revision screen and the capability registration screen. The connector configuration
screen is known, recorded in three places, and will be corrected when there is reason to
prioritise it.

The consequence was stated before the why was given: closed, this work root takes no further
increment, the connector configuration screen stays unable to save, and correcting it later takes
a new initiative, where what this one delivered returns as inventory through the survey of the
code rather than as a reference into this plan.
