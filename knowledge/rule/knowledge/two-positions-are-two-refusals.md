## What it is

A curator fixes positions, not categories, so a refusal that lumped two places into one sentence would be fixed by half.
Counting refusals per position is also what keeps the validation's answer honest — the count answered equals the count produced.
A position is a path, so two distinct paths are two positions, and the same term offended twice at one path cannot happen, because a path points at one place.
This is what makes the same recipient unpublished in both fallbacks two refusals that are told apart, rather than two that are not.

## Rules

A position indexes a hypothesis by its name, and by its ordinal only where that name is not unique.
A validation answers with every refusal its checks produced.
