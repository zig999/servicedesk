- field: statement
  unstated: Whether a surface authoring a new case offers the act submitting it when the surface's own fields omit content create-draft requires — the case's slug and the title, when_to_use, subject and fallback its first version declares — or sends the submission to be refused, and what the surface states in the act's place. contracts/knowledge/case-lifecycle publishes create-draft and contracts/system/case-authoring promises the curator a draft to compose, but no rule, scenario or constraint anywhere states what an authoring surface does before that call is well formed; the knowledge context holds surface rules for a draft already created (a-newly-created-draft-offers-no-act-before-its-own-record-arrives, a-surface-offering-release-states-which-release-conditions-the-draft-meets) and none for the surface that creates it, which the intake's own finding reaches — no case-creation surface exists at all.
  decided: The act that submits a new case is not offered while any content create-draft requires — the case's own slug, and the title, when_to_use, subject and fallback its first version declares — is absent from the surface's own fields, and the surface states in the act's place which of that required content is still absent, named against the field that would carry it. consolidation_register, being optional, never gates the act, and an empty manifest is not an omission. Where every required field holds content, nothing here decides whether the act is offered. Recorded as a new invariant over domain/knowledge/case.
  why: 'The surface can see the same absence the route will refuse — constraints/a-malformed-request-is-refused-with-a-validation-error already answers a body missing a required field with an HTTP 400 VALIDATION_ERROR — so offering the act spends a round trip to tell the curator what the surface is already able to state beside the field, and the outcome being certain on the surface alone is exactly the condition under which this specification has withheld an authoring act once before (rules/integration/a-connector-configuration-surface-offers-no-submission-while-its-content-is-not-well-formed), with its sibling a-connector-configuration-surfaces-readiness-statements-carry-no-claim-no-rule-decides drawing the limit at readings that are not certain — a limit this case does not cross. Deciding it the other way would answer one question two ways across two authoring surfaces of the same specification. The required set is read off the declared elements rather than invented: case declares slug required, case-version declares title, when_to_use, subject and fallback required and consolidation_register optional, and case-lifecycle has the manifest composed after the draft exists. The statement owed in the act''s place follows the same rule''s construction, where the judgment''s own statement stands in the withheld act''s place, and keeps a withheld act readable as a condition the curator can clear. Which control carries the act, its wording and its placement are left undecided deliberately, as four rules of this specification already hold such things to be form belonging to the interface.'
- field: statement
  unstated: Whether a value consisting only of whitespace counts as "absent" for the case's own required-content fields (slug, title, when_to_use, subject, and the fallback's own parts) when deciding whether a case-authoring surface withholds its submitting act. Surfaced by /review-change's specification-conformance pass over connector-configuration-draft-status-response-maps-frontend, evidence at frontend/app/src/services/case-creation-form-schema.ts's isBlank, which already treats a whitespace-only string as absent.
  decided: Absent means holding no content or holding whitespace alone, for every one of this rule's required fields.
  why: rules/integration/a-draft-request-is-offered-only-over-a-named-connector-and-a-chosen-operation already decided this exact question for a connector name field, on the reasoning that a field holding whitespace alone holds no name a curator meant to fill; the same reasoning transfers without a domain difference to answer for, since a case's slug or title holding whitespace alone is equally unfilled from a reader's standpoint. Deciding it the other way would let a curator satisfy this rule's own gate with content nobody can act on.
